# Blender Product Polish

Inspired by product-polish workflows used with Blender MCP.

Do not redesign critical geometry.

## Review in passes

### Silhouette
Does the object read clearly from primary angles?

### Edge language
Are bevel widths consistent with scale?
Are important edges too sharp or too soft?

### Surface/shading
Check normals, smoothing, weighted normals and unwanted pinching.

### Proportion
Look for accidental thickness changes, awkward spacing or visually weak transitions.

### Material
Keep materials coherent and physically plausible.

### Lighting
Use simple key/fill/rim or equivalent lighting to reveal form.
Do not hide bad geometry with dramatic darkness.

### Camera
Use a clear product angle and optional turntable/reveal.

For printable objects, visual polish must not reduce structural thickness or alter mating interfaces.
