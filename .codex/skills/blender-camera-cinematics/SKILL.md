# Blender Camera Cinematics

Use for previewing, presenting or checking an object.

## Composition
Set:
- subject target;
- focal length;
- camera distance;
- elevation;
- framing;
- foreground/background separation.

Avoid fixing composition only by extreme focal lengths.

## Motion patterns
- Orbit: camera circles a subject while tracking it.
- Dolly: translate camera physically rather than only changing focal length.
- Crane: move vertically with controlled target tracking.
- Slow zoom: subtle focal-length change, used sparingly.
- Reveal: move from occluded/close composition to readable whole object.

## Smoothness
Use sparse keyframes and inspect F-curves.
Avoid accidental ease changes or target pops.

For product previews, keep camera motion subordinate to understanding the object.
